# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 5, 2026

Move — Personal Workout Guide is a personal, browser-based workout planning website created by [Christine Paola Ulep](https://christinepaolaulep.vercel.app/).

## Information collected

The project does not require an account and does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The website does not include project-operated analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms, or a server-side database.

## Information stored on your device

The website uses browser local storage to remember:

- completed workout sessions;
- custom workout plans; and
- selected exercises, targets, and related workout settings saved as part of those plans.

This information stays in your browser on your device. It is not transmitted to the project creator. You can remove it by using the website's available reset or delete controls, or by clearing the site's stored browser data.

## Hosting information

If the website is hosted through GitHub Pages, ChatGPT Sites, Vercel, or another hosting provider, that provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, and request logs according to its own privacy policy. This project does not control those provider logs.

## External links

The website or repository may link to external websites. Those services have their own privacy practices, and this policy does not cover their content or data handling.

## Children's privacy

This project is not designed to collect information from children. Because no account or submission system is provided, the project does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

## Policy changes

This policy may be updated if the project's features or data practices change. Updates will be published in this file with a revised effective date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed through [Christine Paola Ulep's website](https://christinepaolaulep.vercel.app/).
